Cinematic progressive pencil line animation on a solid pure bright white background, edge to edge. The very first frame is an entirely empty white field. The illustration draws itself - no artist, no hand, no pen or tool ever appears in the frame at any moment. Static locked-off camera, one continuous 8-second take. The subjects: exactly two small bumper cars seen from the front on a low rink floor, one car on the left and one car on the right, each carrying exactly one seated Korean child holding a round steering wheel with both hands and laughing, and one thin rail line running behind them. Every person is a Korean child with short straight black hair. Each face is drawn with only a few simple lines: two short curved eyes, one small curved mouth, and one soft pink blush on each cheek. The skin, hands and necks are left as completely unpainted white paper with no skin tone of any kind. 0-3.5s: extremely fine soft grey pencil contour lines are traced visibly from endpoint to endpoint - light, feather-soft, barely pressed, each line drawn once, with the gentle wobble of a human hand. No dense cross-hatching, no engraving shading, no heavy outlines; only airy single contour lines. One thin rail line is drawn first, then the rounded shell of the left car, then the right car, then the two seated children and their steering wheels last. Most of every form is left as bare white paper; a few lines simply stop and fade into the white. The composition is asymmetric - the pair of cars sits slightly left of centre. Built progressively, never fading in. 3.5-5.5s: very pale watercolor tints rise softly inside the pencil lines - one pale coral wash on the left car shell, one pale sky-blue wash on the right car shell, soft black on the two children's hair only, pale butter-yellow on one child's clothes and pale sage-mint on the other's, and one single soft yellow watercolor bloom resting loosely behind the cars, spilling a little past the drawn lines. Nothing else is painted. The rink floor and rail stay bare white. Every wash is heavily diluted with water so white paper shows through everywhere, and no area ever becomes dark, dense or fully filled. Generous white margins breathe on all four sides. The whole picture stays bright, warm and airy. 5.5-8s: the two bumper cars nudge gently together and rock once, and both children laugh a little wider. Everything else stays perfectly still.
