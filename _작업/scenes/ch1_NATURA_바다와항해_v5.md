## 장면 A — 「해협을 지나는 고래」  (와)

**단어 10개** `whale` `seal` `dive` `current` `flow` `stream` `channel` `surface` `tide` `spray`

**슬롯 점검**
- A1 주역 = one humpback whale (살아있고 밝음) ✅
- A2 프레임 잘림 = rocky channel wall cropped by the left edge ✅
- A4 스케일 대비 = towering kelp forest in the upper distance ✅
- A 총합 5개 ✅
- B 색은 대상에 붙여 명명 ✅ / C 빛의 사건 1 + 주역 동작 1 + 살아있는 동작 1 ✅
- Never 개수 상한 = more than one whale / more than one seal ✅

```
Cinematic progressive ink-line animation on a solid pure bright white background (#FFFFFF),
edge to edge. The very first frame is an entirely empty white field. Static locked-off
camera, one continuous 8-second take.
The subjects: one humpback whale, a rocky channel wall cropped by the left edge of the
frame, the open tidal channel stretching to a far luminous water surface, one towering
kelp forest in the upper distance, and one slender harbour seal with a rounded head and
short whiskers.
0-3.5s: fine dark-charcoal ink strokes are traced visibly from endpoint to endpoint — each
stroke confident, drawn once. The channel wall's rock strata first, with rich observational
detail, then the tall kelp fronds, then the humpback whale with its long slender pectoral
fins and knobbed rostrum drawn in a few precise strokes. The composition is asymmetric —
the humpback whale sits off-center, small against the vast open channel. Built
progressively, never fading in.
3.5-5.5s: fresh transparent watercolor fills the shapes FROM INSIDE, staying within the ink
lines — clear turquoise and bright aquamarine water full of light, warm golden sand along
the channel floor, fresh olive green on the kelp fronds, the whale's back in soft
silver-blue with a pale cream underside. Every color is bright yet softened as true
watercolor — no primary crayon colors. One consistent light direction from the upper left,
with soft believable shadows. The white background above stays clean.
5.5-8s: a long sparkling path of sunlight appears across the water surface toward the
channel mouth, glittering. The humpback whale glides slowly forward with one gentle
pectoral fin movement, trailing a thin ribbon of spray — and the slender harbour seal
turns once beside it in a graceful shallow dive, then slips back into the current.
Everything else stays still.
Style: fine confident ink drawing with varied line weight and rich observational detail,
fresh luminous watercolor, clear summer daylight, cinematic depth of foreground, middle
ground and far horizon — the feel of a beautiful illustrated atlas for curious teenagers,
never a kindergarten picture book. Museum-quality contemporary illustration.
Never: text, letters, primary crayon colors, muted grey or slate mood, sepia, ink-wash
painting mood, fog, smiling sun, V-shaped birds, rainbows, dead-center composition, paint
splatter, color outside the lines, camera movement, cuts, dissolve, more than one whale,
more than one seal, round or plump bodies, blunt snouts.
```
