## 1. 원본 견본 — 망원경

```
Cinematic progressive line-reveal animation on a solid pure bright white background (#FFFFFF), edge to edge. The very first frame is an entirely empty pure white field. High-key lighting. Static locked-off camera, one continuous 8-second take. The only visible subjects throughout the sequence are the illustrated telescope, stars, constellation lines, crescent moon and restrained watercolor.

0-4s: fine dark-charcoal linework appears progressively from an empty white field. A small elegant telescope and tripod are revealed first through many clean short line segments. Individual stars then appear one by one above it, followed by slender constellation lines and one slim crescent moon. Every detail becomes visible sequentially, never all at once.

4-8s: a highly diluted watercolor wash develops gently behind the constellation in several light transparent layers. Begin with pale powder blue, add a restrained muted indigo near the center, and finish with a faint lavender-gray edge. The white surface remains clearly visible through every wash, with generous untouched white space surrounding the illustration. The stars remain luminous white; the moon receives a delicate silver-blue tint; the telescope receives a subtle cool-gray wash. The color remains airy, translucent and quiet throughout the final frame. Previously revealed lines stay crisp and completely stable. The finished composition contains only one telescope on a tripod, a refined constellation, one crescent moon and a small softly feathered watercolor sky.

Style: delicate fine-line engraving, luminous transparent watercolor, restrained tonal contrast, generous white space, sophisticated museum-quality editorial illustration.
```

### 오른쪽 여백이 뜬 직접 원인
성공문법은 **`equal narrow breathing margins on the left and right` / `both outer edges stay clear`** 로 좌우를 명시한다.
실패 문법의 `gathers compactly toward the center` 는 한쪽으로 뭉치게 만든다.
