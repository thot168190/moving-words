Built progressively, never fading in. 3.5-5.5s: pale watercolor tints rise FROM INSIDE the pencil lines, within the lines — a restrained palette of lagoon turquoise, tangerine, violet, and grassy green, each heavily diluted with water. The larger the area, the lighter and more watery its wash — broad fields, hills and skies receive only the palest, water-flooded tint, far lighter than the small subjects. Color blooms ONLY onto the drawn subjects — all surrounding space stays pure untouched white. The delicate pencil drawing remains the protagonist; color is only a light stain resting on the lines. Generous areas of the paper stay untouched white, and the painting dissolves into untouched white paper well before the frame — soft, wet watercolor boundaries, with generous white margins breathing on at least three sides of the image. One consistent light direction with soft believable shadows. The white background stays pure white. The sky, when present, stays essentially untouched white paper — only the palest breath of tint near the horizon and a few light pencil cloud outlines, never a fully painted sky. 5.5-8s: the gate lifts, the van carries the picnic cart toward the plane, the great wheel turns once, and the aircraft begins a short rolling departure along the island path. Everything else stays perfectly still.
