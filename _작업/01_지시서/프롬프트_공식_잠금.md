# 🔒 검증된 프롬프트 골격 — 「보는 단어장」 장면형 8초 영상

> 잠금: 2026-07-30 · 출처: **대표님이 실제로 뽑아서 채택한 산호 협곡 프롬프트** (scene-ch1-08)
> **골격은 창작하지 않는다. 슬롯만 채운다.**
> 이 파일을 고치려면 대표님 승인이 필요하다. 로부장도 코다리도 임의 수정 금지.

---

## 0. 왜 이 파일이 생겼나

2026-07-30, 로부장이 「폭풍우 치는 만」 프롬프트를 **창작해서** 뽑았고 결과가 실패했다.

| 실패 원인 | 로부장이 쓴 것 | 골격에 있던 것 |
|---|---|---|
| 액자가 생겼다 | `warm paper cream` `paper ground` | **`solid pure bright white background (#FFFFFF), edge to edge`** |
| 그리는 손이 나와 화면을 가렸다 | (배제 문장 없음) | **`The only visible subjects ... are` + 요소 한정** |
| 조감도가 됐다 | `seen from above` | (시점 지시 없음. 깊이 층으로 해결) |
| 먹선이 판화처럼 촘촘 | `nineteenth-century natural-history plate` | `intricate natural-history engraving` (꼬리에만) |
| 물감이 뭉갰다 | `seeps` `soaking` | **`develops in layered depth` + 색 순서 명시** |

**교훈: 검증된 골격이 있으면 창작하지 않는다.**

## 1. 골격 (8블록)

```
[1] 오프닝
Cinematic progressive ink-line construction and {주제}-motion animation on a solid
pure bright white background (#FFFFFF), edge to edge. The very first frame is an
entirely empty pure white field. Static locked-off camera, one continuous 8-second take.

[2] 요소 한정  ← 여기가 폭주를 막는 장치. 절대 빼지 마라
The only visible subjects throughout the sequence are {요소1}, {요소2}, {요소3},
{요소4} and {요소5}.

[3] 0-3.5s  먹선
0-3.5s: fine dark-charcoal ink strokes are visibly traced from one endpoint to the
other across the completely empty white field. {큰 구조}가 forms first through many
short contour and hatching strokes. {중간}이 then {자라나는 방식} from {붙는 곳}.
Finally, {주역}이 is constructed stroke by stroke in {위치}. Each individual stroke
has a clear beginning and ending; the illustration is built progressively rather than
fading into view.

[4] 3.5-5.5s  수채
3.5-5.5s: transparent watercolor develops in layered depth. {색1}이 settles into
{먼 곳} first, followed by {색2} in {중간}, then a small area of {색3} at {가장 깊은
중심}. {주역}이 remains {주역 색} with a narrow untouched white rim. {부속}이 receive
restrained {색}, {색} and {색} accents.

[5] 5.5-8s  움직임  ← 이 2.5초가 단어의 뜻
5.5-8s: {주역}이 {느린 주 동작} with one gentle {세부 움직임}. {부속}이 sway subtly
in {환경 흐름}, while a few tiny {입자}가 drift {방향} at different depths. {배경}과
all fine ink lines remain stable.

[6] 배제  ← 원치 않는 것을 이름으로 부정한다
There is no visible {금지물1} or {금지물2}.

[7] 깊이
Strong foreground, middle-ground and distant layers create the feeling of {공간감}.

[8] 스타일 꼬리  ← 한 글자도 바꾸지 않는다
Style: intricate natural-history engraving, luminous transparent watercolor,
restrained {주제} palette, cinematic depth, graceful realistic motion, sophisticated
museum-quality editorial illustration.
```

## 2. 원본 (산호 협곡 — 채택본)

```
Cinematic progressive ink-line construction and deep-ocean motion animation on a solid
pure bright white background (#FFFFFF), edge to edge. The very first frame is an entirely
empty pure white field. Static locked-off camera, one continuous 8-second take. The only
visible subjects throughout the sequence are one illustrated manta ray, a deep-sea coral
canyon, elegant sea fans, a softly bounded deep-water area and a few restrained
bioluminescent particles. 0-3.5s: fine dark-charcoal ink strokes are visibly traced from
one endpoint to the other across the completely empty white field. A deep-sea canyon forms
first through many short contour and hatching strokes. Tall cold-water branching corals
and elegant sea fans then grow upward branch by branch from the canyon walls. Finally, one
graceful manta ray is constructed stroke by stroke in the open water above them. Each
individual stroke has a clear beginning and ending; the illustration is built progressively
rather than fading into view. 3.5-5.5s: transparent watercolor develops in layered depth.
Pale blue-gray settles into the distant canyon first, followed by restrained teal and
Prussian blue in the middle water, then a small area of muted indigo at the deepest center.
The manta ray remains luminous silver-blue with a narrow untouched white rim. The cold-water
corals receive restrained burgundy, mineral ochre and desaturated sea-green accents. 5.5-8s:
the manta ray glides slowly across the deep open water with one gentle wing movement. The
sea fans sway subtly in a quiet underwater current, while a few tiny blue-white
bioluminescent particles drift upward at different depths. The canyon and all fine ink lines
remain stable. There is no visible water surface or aquarium boundary. Strong foreground,
middle-ground and distant layers create the feeling of a vast deep ocean. Style: intricate
natural-history engraving, luminous transparent watercolor, restrained deep-ocean palette,
cinematic depth, graceful realistic motion, sophisticated museum-quality editorial
illustration.
```

## 3. 왜 이 문장들이 작동하는가

| 문장 | 막아주는 것 |
|---|---|
| `solid pure bright white background (#FFFFFF), edge to edge` | 종이 질감·액자·테두리·크림색 |
| `The very first frame is an entirely empty pure white field` | 이미 그려진 상태로 시작하는 것 |
| `Static locked-off camera, one continuous 8-second take` | 줌·팬·컷 전환 |
| **`The only visible subjects ... are` + 5개 한정** | **손·인물·소품·글자 난입** |
| `visibly traced from one endpoint to the other` | 페이드인 |
| `Each individual stroke has a clear beginning and ending` | 뭉개진 선 |
| `built progressively rather than fading into view` | 페이드인 (이중 방어) |
| `develops in layered depth` + 색 순서 명시 | 물감 뭉갬 · 곰팡이 |
| `a narrow untouched white rim` | 주역이 배경에 묻히는 것 |
| `restrained` (여러 곳) | 색 과포화 |
| `There is no visible ...` | 그 장면에서 특히 잘 나오는 오답 |
| `Strong foreground, middle-ground and distant layers` | 납작한 그림 |
| 스타일 꼬리 6개 항목 | 화풍 이탈 |

---

## 4. 슬롯 채우기 규칙

1. **요소는 정확히 5개.** 4개도 6개도 아니다. 원본이 5개다
2. **먹선 순서는 「큰 구조 → 중간 → 주역」.** 뒤집지 않는다
3. **수채 순서는 「먼 곳 → 중간 → 가장 깊은 중심 → 주역 → 부속」**
4. **움직임은 느린 것 하나 + 흔들림 하나 + 입자 하나.** 셋을 넘기지 않는다
5. **`There is no visible ...`에는 그 장면에서 실제로 잘 나오는 오답을 쓴다**
   - 바다: `water surface or aquarium boundary`
   - 지형: `hand, pen or picture frame` ← 2026-07-30 실패에서 배움
   - 하늘: `ground horizon line or building`
6. **스타일 꼬리는 `restrained {주제} palette`의 주제어만 바꾼다.** 나머지 5개는 고정

| 기획서 v0.1 §4 | |
