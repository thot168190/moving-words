## 프롬프트 (복사해서 그대로 붙여넣기)

```
Cinematic progressive ink-line construction and coastal-storm motion animation on a solid
pure bright white background (#FFFFFF), edge to edge. The very first frame is an entirely
empty pure white field. Static locked-off camera, one continuous 8-second take. The only
visible subjects throughout the sequence are one jutting rocky headland, a rounded inlet
behind it, a narrow strait, one small offshore rock and a low band of drifting mist.
0-3.5s: fine dark-charcoal ink strokes are visibly traced from one endpoint to the other
across the completely empty white field. The curving shoreline forms first through many
short contour and hatching strokes. Windswept grass and layered rock texture then build up
ridge by ridge across the headland. Finally, one small offshore rock is constructed stroke
by stroke beyond the narrow strait. Each individual stroke has a clear beginning and ending;
the illustration is built progressively rather than fading into view. 3.5-5.5s: transparent
watercolor develops in layered depth. Pale blue-gray settles into the far water first,
followed by restrained slate blue in the middle water, then a small area of muted indigo
where the inlet narrows. The headland remains warm gray-ochre with a narrow untouched white
rim. The rock texture receives restrained umber, mineral gray and desaturated moss accents.
5.5-8s: the dark water pushes slowly inward and fills the rounded inlet with one long surge,
then draws a single narrow line of current through the strait. The low mist band spreads
sideways along the far water, while a few tiny white spray flecks lift at different heights
against the headland. The shoreline and all fine ink lines remain stable. There is no
visible hand, pen, picture frame or paper edge. Strong foreground, middle-ground and distant
layers create the feeling of a wide open coast. Style: intricate natural-history engraving,
luminous transparent watercolor, restrained coastal-storm palette, cinematic depth, graceful
realistic motion, sophisticated museum-quality editorial illustration.
```

## 어제 실패본과 무엇이 달라졌나

| | 어제 (실패) | 오늘 (골격 적용) |
|---|---|---|
| 배경 | `warm paper cream` `paper ground` → **액자 생김** | `solid pure bright white (#FFFFFF), edge to edge` |
| 요소 통제 | 없음 → **손이 난입** | `The only visible subjects ... are` 5개 한정 |
| 손 | 배제 안 함 | **`There is no visible hand, pen, picture frame or paper edge`** |
| 시점 | `seen from above` → **조감도** | 시점 지시 없음. 깊이 3층으로 해결 |
| 먹선 | `fine ink linework` (모호) | `visibly traced from one endpoint to the other` + `clear beginning and ending` |
| 물감 | `seeps` `soaking` → **뭉갬** | `develops in layered depth` + 색 3단 순서 명시 |
| 움직임 | 물이 번진다 (모호) | 주 동작 1 + 안개 확산 1 + 물방울 1 |
| 스타일 | 문장을 앞에 길게 | 꼬리 6항목 고정 |

## 판정 기준

| | |
|---|---|
| ① | **첫 프레임이 완전한 흰 화면** |
| ② | **손·펜·액자·종이 테두리가 없다** |
| ③ | 곶이 뾰족하게 튀어나왔다 (cape) |
| ④ | 만이 안쪽으로 굽었다 (bay) |
| ⑤ | 곶과 암초 사이 좁은 물길이 보인다 (channel) |
| ⑥ | 먼 물에 안개띠가 낮게 깔렸다 (fog) |
| ⑦ | 물이 **안쪽으로** 밀려든다 (flood·flow) |
| ⑧ | **산호 협곡과 화풍이 같다** ← 가장 중요 |

⑧이 통과하면 골격이 검증된 것이다. 그러면 남은 146장면을 이 골격으로 찍어낸다.
